DATA
California 2018 ACS 1-Year person data, explicit ACSEmploymentFiltered cohort.
Filters: AGEP > 16, AGEP < 90, PWGTP >= 1. Raw 378817; eligible 302640.
Target: Y = 1 exactly when ESR == 1; otherwise 0 for ESR in 2..6.
Negative is not synonymous with unemployed. ESP removed, people retained.
Inputs (14): AGEP,SCHL,MAR,DIS,MIG,CIT,MIL,ANC,NATIVITY,RELP,DEAR,DEYE,DREM,GCL.
SEX and RAC1P: audit metadata only; neither is a predictor in V0.
ESR, ESP, identifiers, survey weights, and Y are excluded from predictors.
Age standardized; all other inputs one-hot categorical; GCL missingness
retained as NOT_APPLICABLE. Encoder/scaler fitted on reference only.
Encoded dimension: 77. Metrics/training unweighted.
Fixed person-level permutation seed 285; not stratified; not household split.
Split counts: reference30264/train151320/development30264/calibration30264/test60528.
Final test records were not evaluated by the pilots.

TRAINING
MLP: 77 -> 64 -> 32 -> 1, ReLU, BCEWithLogitsLoss; sigmoid for evaluation.
SGD learning rate 0.05, momentum 0, batch size512, epochs20, threshold0.5.
Fixed final epoch; initialization seed285; CPU four threads.
Ordinary SGD: shuffled fixed batches, mean loss normalized by actual batch size.
DP-SGD: Poisson sample rate1/296; expected batch normalizer511; 5920 updates.
Per-example full-model L2 clipping C=1, Gaussian noise, Opacus1.6.0 RDP.
delta=1/(10*151320)=6.608511763150938e-7 for every private run.
DP sampling/noise seeds independently drawn from local entropy; not exported.
secure_mode=False: research RNG, not cryptographically hardened production.

METRICS AND PRIVACY SCOPE
Sex, all nine race codes, and race-by-sex intersections were audited.
Accuracy gap; independence selection-rate gap; separation max(TPRgap,FPRgap);
binary sufficiency max(PPVgap,NPVgap). Undefined rates/gaps remain missing.
Full race separation/sufficiency are undefined for rare groups. No silent drops.
Reference/development/calibration are assumed public auxiliary partitions.
DP applies per model-training run; multiple releases compose. The non-private
baseline and repository/public-data audits are not an end-to-end DP release.
